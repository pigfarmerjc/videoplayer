## Product decisions already approved

- The home experience is a video-only media gallery inspired by iPhone Photos and Google Photos.
- Photos remain available through a separate entry.
- Standalone audio and search are removed.
- Preserve drag-to-scrub with frame-following preview.
- PCM S24 LE playback is a release blocker. MKV/MOV video must remain audible, seekable, track-selectable, and survive full-screen/floating-player round trips.
- Performance with a large video library is a first-class requirement.
